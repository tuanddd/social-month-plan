# Hooks that land in 5 to 10 seconds

Run `hook-writer-sms` for every post: 5 variants, one picked, with one line on why. This page adds patterns that were proven on the first build, plus the shape of a hook-first Short.

## Hook-first Short

| Time | What shows |
|---|---|
| 0 to 1.5 s | The claim as large on-screen text, already there in frame 1. No logo, no intro. |
| 1.5 to 8 s | The proof, shown on real footage. |
| 8 to 10 s | The payoff: the answer, the number, the twist. |
| End | A loop point or a question that invites a comment. Total length 10 to 20 s. |

Sound is on by default, with the subject's own sounds. The caption repeats the claim in words for silent viewers.

## Patterns

| Pattern | Shape | Example from Exact |
|---|---|---|
| Bold claim | Exactly one, never, only | "There is exactly one 3-tile route to this crab." |
| Big number | A computed count that sounds wrong | "36,778 ways to 11. Only 1 uses 4 tiles." |
| Cheesy claim | Over the top, then the proof is small | (none in Exact) "The hardest math in gaming: 2 + 3." |
| Countdown | "You have 5 seconds" | "Find the 3-tile route to make exactly 7." with a 5 s timer |
| POV | Relatable moment | "POV: you're a crab and the total is 7" |
| Debate bait | Two right-sounding answers | "2 + 3 times 2 = ? The crab says 8." |
| Off by one | The near miss | "Off by one. The crab laughed." |
| Self-downplay | Tiny vs huge | "GTA 6 is out. Somebody still has to crack the crabs." |

## Rules

- The claim must be true and in FACTS.md. If you would need to hedge it, pick another hook.
- The on-screen text and the caption must say the same thing as the first frame of the picture. Do not show "just adding" over a minus tile.
- One idea per hook. No questions stacked on questions.
- Write the hook in the tone's voice. Formal hooks are a stat or a result, never a meme.
