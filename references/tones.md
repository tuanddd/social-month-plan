# Tones

The tone sets the knobs below. Write the chosen row, plus any changes the user asked for, into the context file under "Tone".

For free-text tones, start from the nearest row and change only what the words ask for. For example, "chill but nerdy" is Chill with a heavier dev-log pillar.

| Knob | Formal | Professional | Chill | Playful | Meme-ish | Savage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voice | Third person, complete sentences, no slang | "We", clear and direct, light warmth | "We" or "I", short lines, lowercase allowed on X | Puns, the subject's own world and characters | Internet-native, reaction formats | Self-roast, blunt, deadpan |
| Subject vs stories | 70 / 30 | 60 / 40 | 50 / 50 | 50 / 50 | 40 / 60 | 40 / 60 |
| Pillars allowed | Product, proof, insight, news | Plus dev log or behind-the-scenes | Plus fiction | Plus fiction and light memes | Memes and trends as a pillar (15-25%) | Memes 20-30%; the joke is on us |
| Hook patterns | Stat, question, news | Stat, how-I, contrarian | Question, POV, relatable | Cheesy claim, character voice | POV, meme caption, bold claim | Self-downplay, roast, absurd comparison |
| Memes | None | None | 1 or 2, on a trend | 2 or 3 | 4 or more, all different templates | 4 or more, all different templates, punch at ourselves |
| Fiction | None | None | Allowed, labelled | Encouraged, labelled | Allowed, labelled | Allowed, labelled |
| Emoji | None | 0-1 | Sparing | Allowed | Allowed | Sparing (deadpan reads better) |
| CTA | Learn more / visit | Follow / try | Follow, low-key | Character asks | "Follow anyway" | Self-aware ask ("not out yet, follow anyway") |
| Platform lean | LinkedIn, X | LinkedIn, X, YouTube | X, Bluesky, Shorts | Shorts, TikTok, X | X, TikTok, Reels, Shorts | X, TikTok, Shorts |

## Savage and meme-ish, in practice

- Downplay the subject on purpose. The template is: "[huge thing everyone cares about] vs [our tiny thing]".
  - Example: an Expanding Brain meme whose final panel is "Adding 2 + 3 on a yellow handheld with a crank".
  - Example: a Gru's Plan where the plan is to "make a game that is just adding numbers" and then "win Game of the Year".
- Stay true while being savage. "Not nominated. Watching anyway." works because it is true.
- Every meme uses a different classic template on its original image. See `trends-and-memes.md`.
- Never punch at the audience, a named creator or another studio's people. A big event or brand can be the backdrop (the crowd in a crowd-vs-us template); the joke stays on us.

## Formal and professional, in practice

- No meme pillar and no in-world fiction.
- Stories around the subject become case studies, numbers, decisions and process.
- Hooks lead with a fact or a result.
- Threads are numbered, with one idea per post. LinkedIn posts run 1,200-1,500 characters with 3-5 hashtags.
